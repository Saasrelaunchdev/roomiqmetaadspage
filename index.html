    Where does the data come from?

    Observed listing data, room by room: weekly asking rents, move-in fees, room occupancy and listing status, rolled up by property, submarket and market. Thin areas are labeled Thin.

    What does it cost?

    Free is $0 with no card: one main market and 3 estimates a month. Pro is $45 a month, or $450 a year with two months free.

    Follow-ups

    Can I analyze a house I haven't bought yet?

    Yes, that's what it's built for. Enter the address and what you know: bedrooms, baths, which rooms get a private bath. No ownership or rental history needed.

    Can I use it on houses I already run?

    Yes. Benchmark them against observed rooms nearby: occupancy, asking rent and estimated revenue over 7, 30 and 90 days, by bath type and size.

    I already use a deal analyzer or a market data pass. Why add RoomIQ?

    Neither prices the rooms in the house you're looking at. RoomIQ adds room-level rent, occupancy and nearby supply for one address, with a confidence label, then the same deal math.

    Results and expectations

    Will the estimate be what I actually earn?

    No tool can promise that, and RoomIQ doesn't. The estimate uses observed asking rents and occupancy near the address, not collected payments, and labels its support and confidence.

    What does Confidence Low mean?

    The evidence behind that estimate is thin: few comps, or comps far from the address. RoomIQ shows how far they are, so you can weigh the number.

    How does the Free plan differ from the Pro trial?

    Free needs no card: one main market, 90 days of history, 5 Analyzer opens and 3 estimates a month. At a Free limit you can start a 7-day Pro trial, card required.

    Objections

    Will my own houses show up on this site?

    No. In public, including on this site, RoomIQ never shows a host's name, a listing title, a listing photo or an exact address. Every house in our demos is made up.

    Is this financial or legal advice?

    No. RoomIQ is informational. It doesn't check zoning, arrange financing or insurance, or tell you a deal is good. Ask the city, a lender and an insurer.

    What if Pro isn't for me?

    Cancel before the 7-day trial ends to avoid billing. The Free plan stays free, with no card.

    Who is RoomIQ not for?

    Whole-home, nightly or flip investors, anyone looking for deals to buy, anyone who needs a property manager, and anyone who wants a number that makes promises.

    Straight about the limits

    What RoomIQ doesn't do

    It's one layer: the evidence for the house. These parts stay with you.

    • Zoning. Rules on unrelated adults sharing a home differ by city. Ask the city or an attorney.
    • Financing. Many loans use the appraised whole-house rent. Talk early to a lender who knows room rentals.
    • Insurance. Not a given. The one rent-by-the-room program we found excludes Houston and the Dallas-Fort Worth counties.
    • Deal sourcing. No off-market lists or lead lists. You bring the address.
    • Property management. It doesn't place renters or run houses.
    • Listing photos. Upgrade concepts are watermarked and for planning, never for advertising a room.
